About me

My story, in a nutshell

🔋 A physicist between matter and computing

After my Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Physics at the University of Bologna and a PhD in Condensed Matter Physics, I now work in the world of High Performance Computing: I help researchers and scientists harness supercomputers to understand matter, one atom at a time.

🌎 A citizen of the world

I have lived and studied in several countries: a research stay in the United States at Brown University, and an Erasmus in Spain. Every experience left something behind — a language, a method, a new perspective.

⛰️ A bit of nature

When I'm not in front of a screen, you'll find me on a trail: running in the mountains is how I recharge my batteries. Then a good book, a good meal, and the day is perfect.

✨ My philosophy

"Curiosity keeps pushing me in search of new experiences." Whether it's a quantum material never studied before or a summit I've never climbed, for me life is exploration.
